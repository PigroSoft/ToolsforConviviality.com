Kdenlive

A free multi-track video editor from the KDE community.

Instead of Adobe Premiere Pro

M · ToolOpen sourceFreeNo accountWorks offline

Screenshot from kdenlive.org, taken 2026-10-23, shown to help you recognise the tool. It belongs to its maker. Ask us to remove it.

Kdenlive is free and open-source video editing software under the GNU GPL, developed by the KDE community, a nonprofit, with Jason Wood having started it in 2002. It has been funded through community drives and, in 2025, an NLnet grant for a new dopesheet feature.

It handles serious editing work with multiple tracks and no watermark or subscription, and exports to standard video formats. Video is heavy on hardware, and the interface is busier than Premiere's. It is a good fit for people who want to own their editing tools.

    What stands out

  • Multi-track video editing with no watermark or subscription
  • Developed by the KDE community, started in 2002
  • Exports to standard video formats
